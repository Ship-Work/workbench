# Overnight batch · 15 October 2026

The record of one unattended lead session on main `edc15df` (Workbench 2.5.0 plus the two Persona follow-ups). The maintainer asked for real, relevant, non-bloat improvements made build-ready on GitHub, then built and installed in Preview for him to test. This file keeps what was considered, what was built, and what was deliberately not built with the condition that would change each decision. Method: [the release-lead playbook](../release-lead.md).

## How the work was chosen

- **Request history.** The maintainer's own Claude and Codex requests from 1 September to 14 October were reduced privately to 47 themes, each with a count of sessions, first and last dates and an outcome. Most of his repeated asks (the floating pill, one coherent app, dictation that lands, Meetings he can copy from, Snap & Talk handoff, Present) are **shipped in source but never confirmed by him in the installed app**. Asks stop where a real check happened. Only themes enter this repository.
- **Backlog audit.** 36 open issues were traced to their landing commits. 26 have their source on main and owe only native acceptance; #149 and #220 are superseded; #60 and #64 need a maintainer decision; #15, #63, #76, #150 and #287 still had real source work. The summary is on #7.
- **Defect hunts.** Five journey hunters (Dictate, Snap and Snap & Talk, Meetings and History, Presenting, window and setup) and two visual reviewers of the 350-render surface gallery looked for concrete user-visible failures with file:line evidence. A leftover-QA checker rechecked the open P3s from the 8 October sweep.
- **Verification.** 65 raw candidates were merged to 58, and one independent skeptic per candidate tried to refute it on main. 47 survived, 11 were rejected. All survivors are **Quality** under the [Grammar](../workbench.md#grammar): no new capability, place, setting group or entry point.
- **A second round, from new angles.** Saved work across quit, crash and update; accessibility and larger text; the dark and narrow renders; Persona, Timer and Library; a whole-app names and doors audit; periodic main-thread work; error text that reaches people. Each finding was deduplicated against round one. 13 survived verification, but only three scored 3 (noticeable friction in a common path), against nineteen at 3 or 4 in round one, and none scored higher. The source-provable defect yield is close to dry at this commit. More value now depends on the maintainer's own use of the installed build and on the hardware checks below, not on another source sweep.

## What was built

The reviews caught two problems before they reached the installed build. Faster prompt insertion overflowed a shift and crashed on prompts longer than about 14,000 characters (fixed in #344, with a check at the 50,000-character Library limit). ## Round three: delight and code-base value · morning of 15 October

Asked for one more turn "re delighting the use and delighting the value of the code base" before testing and shipping, the lead ran a third discovery on the train head `14d06bc`. It used seven delight hunters (Dictate, Snap, Meetings and History, presenting, the window, visual polish from fresh renders of the train, copy voice) and four code-base hunters (CI flakiness, compiler warnings, dead code, contributor friction), with one skeptic per candidate.

**Yield.** 35 survived, but only four scored 3 and none higher; 20 were rejected. One value-2 item mattered more than its score and was built first: Light cleanup, the default text style, deleted real acronyms (ERM, HMM, UM, UH) and mangled "Uh-huh" (#364). A reported Save failure from an empty Snap tag turned out unreachable from the editor, which already drops empty tags, so it became a guard check instead of a change (#366). **The source-provable yield is exhausted at this commit**: three rounds from different angles went from nineteen value-3+ items to three to four.

**CI and flakes, measured.** Two checks written tonight failed only on the CI runner: a ⌘H check that yielded once before asserting a main-queue notification, and a 250 ms relayout budget that a shared runner exceeded at 252 ms. Both were fixed with bounded waits or a budget that still fails the old code twentyfold. The round's flake hunter then predicted five more classes in older checks. The skeptics found none had failed or hung in the last 200 CI runs, each with measured margin, so they were not changed. The trigger is any one of them failing once, then fixing that one with its evidence.

Built in five PRs stacked on the train, each independently reviewed: #365 (Snap & Talk adds Snaps in capture order and keeps its Capture area), #366 (a new caption starts selected so typing replaces "Your text"; the Line width slider and Snap search say what they do; tagged cards stay level), #367 (History's Copy and Copy result confirm where pressed and VoiceOver hears it; Settings › Updates keeps its confirmation under the row), #364 (Light cleanup keeps acronyms; Dictate shows download progress; aligned empty-transcript hint; Home's first result in a visible box; steady Settings switcher) and #363 (CI timeout headroom for the surface gallery, the stale key-window test, and the build's warnings except two left deliberately in Present and the handoff check).

## Wave four and the code-base wave · afternoon of 15 October

The maintainer's goal said to stop only when no more value could be delivered, so the value-2 items round three had deferred only for file ownership were built once those files were free. They went into five PRs stacked on the train and merged into it: #371 (Sound Settings… after two silent dictations; VoiceOver hears where a dictation's words went; "1 word"; shortcut hints only while the key works; "Finish dictation" and "Start dictating"; "Export current text…" with filenames that say which meeting), #370 (Home shows speech setup as one live row; the sample deck cover keeps its size), #372 (the Timer keeps the display awake while a countdown shows, for at most five minutes after Time is up and never while paused, hidden or minimised; Present's footer caveat; error lines as primary words beside an orange symbol; VoiceOver names for the panel's Options menus), #369 (a call's purpose ends with its recording, so a later microphone-only meeting saves as a Meeting) and #368 (an unsaved Arrange overlays layout survives leaving Persona, comes back only when you reopen Arrange overlays, and is dropped with a notice if its group's members changed).

Two of those were in Persona (the kept layout took over every visit, and it was never rechecked against its group) and were fixed before install. The rest were wording and doc drift, also fixed.

The seven verified dead-code retirements were built as three PRs stacked after the train and deliberately **not** merged into it, so the build under test does not change: #376 (Home's phantom Open Home… route, two Not now buttons that never render, the toolbar More and Appearance leftovers), #375 (the unopened in-place shortcut recorder and declarations nothing references) and #373 (Read's leftovers, Meetings helpers nothing called, and the old handoff-folder route).
