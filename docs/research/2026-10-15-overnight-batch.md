# Overnight batch · 15 October 2026

The record of one unattended lead session on main `edc15df` (Workbench 2.5.0 plus the two Persona follow-ups). The maintainer asked for real, relevant, non-bloat improvements made build-ready on GitHub, then built and installed in Preview for him to test. This file keeps what was considered, what was built, and what was deliberately not built with the condition that would change each decision. Method: [the release-lead playbook](../release-lead.md).

## How the work was chosen

- **Request history.** The maintainer's own Claude and Codex requests from 1 September to 14 October were reduced privately to 47 themes, each with a count of sessions, first and last dates and an outcome. Most of his repeated asks (the floating pill, one coherent app, dictation that lands, Meetings he can copy from, Snap & Talk handoff, Present) are **shipped in source but never confirmed by him in the installed app**. Asks stop where a real check happened. Only themes enter this repository.
- **Backlog audit.** 36 open issues were traced to their landing commits. 26 have their source on main and owe only native acceptance; #149 and #220 are superseded; #60 and #64 need a maintainer decision; #15, #63, #76, #150 and #287 still had real source work. The summary is on #7.
- **Defect hunts.** Five journey hunters (Dictate, Snap and Snap & Talk, Meetings and History, Presenting, window and setup) and two visual reviewers of the 350-render surface gallery looked for concrete user-visible failures with file:line evidence. A leftover-QA checker rechecked the open P3s from the 8 October sweep.
- **Verification.** 65 raw candidates were merged to 58, and one independent skeptic per candidate tried to refute it on main. 47 survived, 11 were rejected. All survivors are **Quality** under the [Grammar](../workbench.md#grammar): no new capability, place, setting group or entry point.
- **A second round, from new angles.** Saved work across quit, crash and update; accessibility and larger text; the dark and narrow renders; Persona, Timer and Library; a whole-app names and doors audit; periodic main-thread work; error text that reaches people. Each finding was deduplicated against round one. 13 survived verification, but only three scored 3 (noticeable friction in a common path), against nineteen at 3 or 4 in round one, and none scored higher. The source-provable defect yield is close to dry at this commit. More value now depends on the maintainer's own use of the installed build and on the hardware checks below, not on another source sweep.

## What was built

Seven streams, then two more from round two, one worktree and one worker each (a tenth PR, #339, was closed; see the ledger). Every stream had an independent adversarial reviewer before its PR, and the integrated branch had a second, cross-stream review. The reviews caught two problems before they reached the installed build. Faster prompt insertion overflowed a shift and crashed on prompts longer than about 14,000 characters (fixed in #344, with a check at the 50,000-character Library limit). And a narration kept at Quit failed at the next launch because Snap & Talk drained its queue before speech had loaded; #345 now holds the take queued until speech is ready, with a check that uses a real engine still preparing.
