## Release notes

# Workbench 2.7.0

Press Finish and your meeting is already written down: copy it and go.

- **Finish, then Copy.** Meetings confirms your call's words a phrase at a time while you talk, so Finish saves the transcript in about half a second instead of transcribing the whole call again (a 15-minute call used to take 12 seconds). The words are as accurate as before, and twice as accurate when your microphone hears the call through your speakers.
- **Your words stay yours on laptop speakers.** The other side's voice coming back through your microphone is no longer saved as "You", and quick replies like "Yes" keep every word. Short pauses no longer split what you said into new paragraphs.
- **Dictate straight after an update.** After Workbench or macOS updates, speech needs about 15 seconds to get ready. Dictate now starts recording at once and writes your words when you stop, instead of sending you to Models.
- **Live words, sooner.** Dictation shows your first words about half a second after you speak, down from a second and a half.
- **Meetings take half the space.** Saved recordings are kept in Apple Lossless once their transcript is saved: every sample identical, about 400 MB an hour for a two-sided call instead of 800 MB. Recordings you already have are compressed quietly in the background.

Also in this release:

- **Safari and FaceTime calls.** Meetings offers a Safari call when macOS can tell it apart, and says so when it can't, before anything records. FaceTime and relayed iPhone calls record the call's audio.
- **Long meetings say when they'll finish.** From 1 h 55 m the page, menu-bar panel and Home say when Workbench will finish the meeting, and a meeting Workbench ends itself says so and why in its notes.
- **Follow-ups you can trust.** A failed follow-up runs again from the meeting; one that was stopped is never sent twice.
- **Snap's region crosshair appears a quarter of a second sooner.**

When you're ready, update from General › Updates. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.

## Known limits

- **Meetings' two-sided capture is not attested.** System Audio Recording was silent for both editions on the maintainer's Mac on 15 October, so a call's other side cannot be shown recorded until it is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording and a real call is recorded (M1, M2 in #7). The Finish timings above were measured on synthetic two-track meetings played through the app's own capture path, not a real call.
- **What this cut attests, on the exact notarized package:** the fresh smoke (opening, permissions by unchanged signing identity, saved work) and the signed 2.6.0-to-2.7.0 upgrade (scripts/release/config.json, bounded-slice). The maintainer asked to "Run all PRs to Production - I will test what truely needs me there", so D1 and the other foundation journeys stay with him in production, reported through Report a Problem.
- **Going back to 2.6.0** keeps every meeting readable: 2.6.0 plays Apple Lossless originals through the same AVFoundation path. Snap & Talk's earlier note stands: let every new take reach Ready before going back to 2.5.0 or earlier.
