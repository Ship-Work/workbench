## Release notes

# Workbench 2.7.0

Press Finish and your meeting is already written down; moments later it has a name. Copy it and go.

- **Finish, then Copy.** Meetings confirms your call's words a phrase at a time while you talk, so Finish saves the transcript in about half a second instead of transcribing the whole call again (a 15-minute call used to take 12 seconds). The words are as accurate as before.
- **Every meeting has a name.** Shortly after Finish, Apple Intelligence names the meeting and says in a line what it was about, with the decisions and next steps worth finding again. History, Home and Review transcript lead with that name; History has a Meetings filter and reads by day; and copying a meeting starts with its name, app, time and length, so meetings pasted together stay apart. Naming happens on your Mac and never changes your words; rename one in Details…, or turn naming off in Meetings' Recording options. Needs Apple Intelligence; otherwise meetings are named by their app.
- **Your words stay yours on laptop speakers.** The other side's voice coming back through your microphone is now matched to the call and kept out of "You"; when it isn't clear, nothing is dropped. Quick replies like "Yes" keep every word, and short pauses no longer split what you said into new paragraphs.
- **Dictate sooner, even straight after an update.** Your first live words show about 0.6 seconds after you start speaking, down from nearly 1.5. After Workbench or macOS updates, speech needs about 15 seconds to get ready: Dictate now starts recording at once and writes your words once speech is ready, instead of sending you to Models.
- **Meetings take half the space.** Saved recordings are kept in Apple Lossless once their transcript is saved, every sample identical: an hour of a two-sided call should take about 400 MB instead of 800 MB. Recordings you already have are compressed quietly in the background.

Also in this release:

- **Follow up on any meeting.** Prepare follow-up… is on every meeting in History, not only the newest, and ⌘F finds words in Review transcript.
- **Safari and FaceTime calls.** Meetings offers a Safari call when macOS can tell it apart, and says so when it can't, before anything records. FaceTime and relayed iPhone calls record the call's audio.
- **Long meetings say when they'll finish.** From 1 h 55 m the page, menu-bar panel and Home say when Workbench will finish the meeting, and a meeting Workbench ends itself says so and why in its notes.
- **Follow-ups you can trust.** A failed follow-up can be run again from its meeting; one you stopped isn't re-sent unless you choose Retry… in History.
- **Snap's region crosshair appears a quarter of a second sooner.**

When you're ready, update from General › Updates. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.

## Known limits

- **Meetings' two-sided capture is not attested.** System Audio Recording was silent for both editions on the maintainer's Mac on 15 October, so a call's other side cannot be shown recorded until it is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording and a real call is recorded (M1, M2 in #7). The Finish timings above were measured on synthetic two-track meetings fed through the app's meeting owner and live pass by a playback stand-in at four times real time, not a real call.
- **What this cut attests, on the exact notarized package:** the fresh smoke (opening, permissions by unchanged signing identity, saved work) and the signed 2.6.0-to-2.7.0 upgrade (scripts/release/config.json, bounded-slice). The maintainer asked to "Run all PRs to Production - I will test what truely needs me there", so D1 and the other foundation journeys stay with him in production, reported through Report a Problem.
- **Meeting names need macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence** turned on in System Settings. Without it, meetings are named by their app ("Zoom meeting") and Recording options says why. Naming has been measured on synthetic transcripts on an M5 (3–4 s for 570–930 words, 8.3 s for 2,331 words, about 15 minutes; about half a minute an hour by extrapolation), not yet on a real call, and it waits while Dictate, Meetings or Snap & Talk is listening or transcribing.
- **Going back to 2.6.0** loses names you typed in Details… the next time 2.6.0 saves History; names written on your Mac come back when you return to 2.7.0. It keeps every meeting readable: 2.6.0 plays Apple Lossless originals through the same AVFoundation path. Snap & Talk's earlier note stands: let every new take reach Ready before going back to 2.5.0 or earlier.
