## Release notes

# Workbench 2.6.0

Fewer surprises: every dictation, Snap and meeting now ends with your words kept and a plain answer about where they went.

- **Snaps stay sharp and easy to fix.** Screen captures keep every Retina pixel. A new caption starts selected, so typing replaces "Your text", and Undo steps back one whole edit, not one keystroke. Copy says "Image copied" where you pressed it.
- **Dictation always tells you what happened.** If a live insertion stops early or you cancel after words have landed, your words stay copied and the result says so where you acted, with Retry beside it. Light cleanup no longer deletes acronyms like ERM or UM, or mangles "Uh-huh".
- **Meetings hold up on long calls.** A long call stays responsive with its page open and always saves. Plugging in headphones restarts only the audio that changed, so your microphone keeps recording. If no sound arrives from the call app, Meetings says why and where to allow it. ⌘Q while recording asks first.
- **Snap & Talk keeps your take.** Quitting mid-narration keeps what you said and transcribes it once speech is ready. A retake that hears nothing keeps your earlier words, and Snaps you add join in the order you captured them.
- **Keys that tell the truth.** A shortcut another app is holding shows as unavailable, and clears by itself once that app lets go. Every Change shortcut… opens Keyboard on that shortcut.

Also in this release:

- **Prepare follow-up… offers the Meeting notes and follow-up skill** first, on the runner you've set up, and a meeting is handed off once.
- **Presenting is quieter.** Present, Draw and Persona successes stop pinning orange lines in the menu-bar panel. The Timer keeps the display awake only while a countdown shows. Board export is reachable while a board is up.
- **Updates and installs explain themselves.** An update that fails says why and what to do, and long prompts insert in seconds.
- **A fresh install reads as ready to set up**, not as a failed speech setup, and History's Copy and Copy result confirm beside the button.

When you're ready, update from General › Updates. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.

## Known limits

- **Meetings' two-sided capture is not attested.** The real-capture check added in #374 found System Audio Recording silent for both editions on the maintainer's Mac on 15 October, so a call's other side cannot be shown recorded until it is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording and a real call is recorded (M1, M2 in #7). Device switching during a real call also still needs hardware acceptance.
- **What this cut attests, on the exact notarized package:** the fresh smoke (opening, permissions by unchanged signing identity, saved work) and the signed 2.5.0-to-2.6.0 upgrade (scripts/release/config.json, bounded-slice, #390). Asked for a two-minute dictation check before publication, the maintainer answered "publish please … I will test in production", so D1 joins the journeys he tests in production and reports through Report a Problem. Everything 2.5.0 listed as not newly certified still stands.
- **The opening observation ended early.** The app opened, signalled ready and was still running 26 s later. At 23:25:10Z a click quit it cleanly (exit status 0, no report) while another agent, Codex Computer Use, was operating the desktop.
- **Going back to 2.5.0** is unsupported while a Snap & Talk take is Queued, Transcribing or Failed: 2.5.0 would overwrite that section's earlier notes. Let every new take reach Ready first (acceptance-evidence/storage-compatibility.md).
