# Keys, Present, Timer and the gallery: a refused key shows as refused, Reset layout keeps My device, Duration shows the choice, the gallery keeps its own voice settings

Branch `claude/keys-present-timer-gallery`, stacked on the 15 October release train (`claude/train-1015`, on top of `main` at `edc15df`). The checks below ran on the branch's final source. Synthetic data only: the StageKit tests use temporary folders and scratch preference suites, and the surface gallery uses its isolated home. No installed app, saved scene, Persona choice, shortcut or privacy setting was used or changed.

## What changed

1. **A key macOS refused at launch shows as refused in Keyboard and Home** (#76). The keyboard coach was built before the first Voice registration, and only practice ending, the panel opening or a door refreshed it. A key another app already held when Workbench launched stayed in Home's Your keys and in Settings › Keyboard with no problem, and Practice ended with "Ready to use anywhere". `registerShortcuts()` now refreshes the coach's catalogue after every registration, the launch one included. Its suspend guard already stops it while a shortcut is recorded or practised, so the separate refresh after one ended is gone.
2. **Present's Reset layout keeps the saved My device** (#276). Scene options › Reset layout wrote the generic phone and literal copies of a new scene's position, zoom and height. `DemoScenes.resetLayout()` now resets the selected scene to a fresh scene's layout in `newSceneViewport`: the saved My device, or the phone when none is saved. That is the shape the three ways of making a scene already used, and they now read the same property. Name, logo, hand, Persona, ambience and the device switch stay as they were. The button's label and style are unchanged.
3. **Timer: Duration shows the duration that is set, and the chime has one name** (#174). The Timer menu (the panel's Timer Options and Home's live row) checked a Duration only when it matched one of its presets, so the Timer page's own 3 min, or a typed 20 or 2.5, left nothing checked. That duration now appears checked in its place among the six unchanged presets, worded as the page's field shows it. A duration that reads as a preset, such as 5.0000001, checks that preset rather than adding a second "5 min". The chime switch read "Play a chime when time is up" on the page and "Chime When Finished" in the menu; they now read "Chime when time is up" and "Chime When Time Is Up", as the legacy controls already did. The registry renames the chime entry and records the Duration readback as a runtime Timer option.
4. **The gallery keeps React to my voice in its own store** (#381). The app's voice access read and wrote the ring's choice and colour in the edition's named stage domain directly, while every other stage preference lives in the store the controller is built with. `PersonaVoiceAccess.system(defaults:)` now takes the store, and the coordinator passes its settings' own. In the app that is the same stage domain, so saved choices stay where they were. The surface gallery's isolated store now decides the Persona page's switch, as it does on CI. The gallery seeds that store with a voice colour no preset has, leaving the switch off, and checks that Persona started with it.

The new gallery step registers through `AppDelegate.registerShortcuts()` on ⌃⌥⇧⌘F13 to F20, never the person's keys or the ⌃⌥⇧ keys `--check-input` uses. It holds Dictate's key with a second registration in the same process, then checks that Keyboard's Dictate entry says it is in use, that Home neither treats it as usable nor suggests it, and that Practice does not start on it. It releases the key, registers again and checks that the problem clears. It runs in the light pass only, since both passes run at once, and puts back the preferences, failures, Keyboard entries and selection it changed. Another gallery on the same Mac can hold those keys for a moment, so a failure is tried again up to four times, a quarter of a second apart, before it counts.

The Persona renders from the final source are byte-identical to the committed `after/` ones, and the voice-refused and timer-window renders are still byte-identical to `main`'s, so the seeded colour changes nothing drawn. The stage domain's `persona.voiceRing` still read 1 after every run.
